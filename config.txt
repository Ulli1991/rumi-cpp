						#   particle's kicks <= this x |E0| per step (code default 0 = off: in small systems such as
						#   16 stars, |E0| is tiny and the step collapses). 200k, 10 % binaries, cm off: |dE/E| 1.5e-8 -> 3e-10
						#   (old seed-7 ICs), 1.2e-9 -> 2e-11 (seed 13), at equal or lower s/step
